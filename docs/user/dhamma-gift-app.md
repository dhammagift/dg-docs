The Dhamma.Gift app for iPhone, iPad and Android: search and read the Pāli
Canon — Suttas and Vinaya in Pāli with Russian and English translations —
with or without an internet connection. Free, no ads, no tracking, no
account needed.

## Offline library

Download the library once and everything works in airplane mode: search,
the table of contents, the texts with their translations, and the
dictionary. You choose what goes on the device: Settings → Offline library
→ **Choose…**. The Mahāsaṅgīti is required (it holds the contents and
search); the other Pali editions and the translation languages are up to
you, and the texts you leave out open online. Voices can be downloaded too,
so reading aloud also works offline ([Voice](/tts)). The download keeps
going in the background and with the screen locked. Until then the app
works online, exactly like the site. When the library has new files, the
**Offline library update** bar offers **Update**. **Change…** in the same row
changes the choice or deletes the library (**Remove all**) any time.

## Search and reader

Full-text search over the whole canon, right on the device: infix matches
(`kacchapa` finds `mahākacchapānaṁ`), diacritics optional, filters by
collection and translator, and a match context that opens straight in the
reader ([Search](/search-guide)). In the reader: a text beside its
translation, reading modes, Pāli in other scripts. Tap a Pāli word — the
[dictionary](/dictionary) opens, offline too. Tap a paragraph — it is read
aloud ([Voice](/tts)). The app opens where you left off: the last search, a
text, the contents or Memo (Settings → On start, open).

## Built into the phone

- **Share to Dhamma.Gift.** Select a word or a passage in any other app or
  site → Share → Dhamma.Gift, and the search results open right away. On
  iPhone they appear in the share sheet itself, without leaving the app you
  were in. A shared dhamma.gift link opens that text.
- **Shortcuts on the icon.** A long press on the app icon. On Android:
  favorites & history, plus either your 3 most recent texts or Contents,
  Memo and Dictionary (Settings → Recent texts in app shortcuts). On
  iPhone: favorites & history and your 3 most recent texts; Siri and the
  Shortcuts app can open Dhamma.Gift, a section (Contents, Memo,
  Dictionary…) or a search.
- **In the text-selection menu.** Select text in any app, and
  Dhamma.Gift in the selection menu opens the search. *Android.*
- **The phone's own search.** In Android's system search Dhamma.Gift is
  offered as a place to search: the query opens as a search in the app. The
  texts you read recently show up there too. *Android.*
- **Quick Settings and Control Center.** A Dhamma.Gift button in the
  notification shade on Android and in Control Center on iPhone: one tap
  from anywhere, offline.
- **Player on the lock screen.** While a text is read aloud or a recording
  plays (the Pātimokkha's sections), its player is in the notification shade
  and on the lock screen. *Android.*
- **Links open in the app.** A dhamma.gift link tapped in a messenger or in
  notes (`https://dhamma.gift/mn1`) opens the text in the app, not in the
  browser.
- **Reminders on schedule.** [Subscriptions](/quickmodal) — a text or a word
  to come back to — can remind you at a set time with a notification; a
  browser can't promise that with the tab closed.

## Updates

Fixes reach the app from the site, without a store update: when new files
have come in, the **New version available** bar offers **Update** (the page
reloads) or **Later**. Settings → **App version** shows the version; its
**check** button looks for an update, says the result in a word or two (Up
to date, Update available, Offline…) and then offers the next step:
**Update**, **Retry**, or **Store** when the update needs a newer version
of the app.

## Your data

Favorites, history, notes and settings stay on the device. Signing in is
optional and only syncs them between your devices ([Login](/login)).

## Other ways to install

- **Windows 10 and 11:** Dhamma.Gift in the
  [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NK892G5P5GT) — the site in its own
  window, always the current version.
- **[PWA](/pwa)**: the site installed from the browser, with an icon,
  shortcuts and the same offline library (not in a private window).
