The Dhamma.Gift app for iPhone, iPad and Android: search and read the Pāli
Canon — Suttas and Vinaya in Pāli with Russian and English translations —
with or without an internet connection. Free, no ads, no tracking, no
account needed.

## Offline library

Download the whole library once (about 216 MB) and everything works in
airplane mode: search, the table of contents, every text with its
translations, and the dictionary. The download keeps going in the
background and with the screen locked. Until then the app works online,
exactly like the site. The library can be deleted any time: Settings →
Offline library.

## Search and reader

Full-text search over the whole canon, right on the device: infix matches
(`kacchapa` finds `mahākacchapānaṁ`), diacritics optional, filters by
collection and translator, and a match context that opens straight in the
reader ([Search](/search-guide)). In the reader: a text beside its
translation, reading modes, Pāli in other scripts. Tap a Pāli word — the
[dictionary](/dictionary) opens, offline too. Tap a paragraph — it is read
aloud ([Voice](/tts)).

## Built into the phone

- **Share to Dhamma.Gift.** Select a word or a passage in any other app or
  site → Share → Dhamma.Gift, and the search results open right away. On
  iPhone they appear in the share sheet itself, without leaving the app you
  were in. A shared dhamma.gift link opens that text.
- **Shortcuts on the icon.** A long press on the app icon: table of
  contents, favorites & history, Memo, dictionary, and the texts you read
  last. On iPhone the same actions are available to Siri and the Shortcuts
  app.
- **The phone's own search.** Type a sutta number or its name (`sn56.11`,
  `dhammacakkappavattana`, diacritics optional) in the system search — the
  text is found there and opens in the app. *Android; iOS (Spotlight) —
  coming.*
- **Quick Settings / Control Center.** A Dhamma.Gift button in the
  notification shade: one tap from anywhere, offline. *Android; iOS
  (Control Center) — coming.*
- **Links open in the app.** A dhamma.gift link tapped in a messenger or in
  notes (`https://dhamma.gift/mn1`) opens the text in the app, not in the
  browser.
- **Reminders on schedule.** [Subscriptions](/quickmodal) — a text or a word
  to come back to — can remind you at a set time with a notification; a
  browser can't promise that with the tab closed.

## Your data

Favorites, history, notes and settings stay on the device. Signing in is
optional and only syncs them between your devices ([Login](/login)).

## Other ways to install

- **APK for Android without Google Play:**
  [dg-app-full releases](https://github.com/dhammagift/dg-app-full/releases/latest)
  (`app-debug.apk`). Android asks to confirm an install from outside Google
  Play.
- **[PWA](/pwa)** — the site installed from the browser: an icon and
  shortcuts, but no offline library.
