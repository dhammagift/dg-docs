Compare: Pali Editions Side by Side

Any text can be read in six Pali editions at once, line by line, with the differences highlighted.

TagEditionType of editionSource
SRSiam Rath 1927national, ThailandE-Tipitaka
BJTBuddha Jayanti (1957-1993)national, Sri LankaTipitaka.lk
VRIChaṭṭha Saṅgāyana (Sixth Council, 1954-1956)national, MyanmarTipitaka.org
MS05Mahāsaṅgīti 2005, as printedderived: the Sixth Council edition (Myanmar) in Roman scriptTipitaka2500.github.io
MSMahāsaṅgītiderived from MS05: SuttaCentral's segments and editsSuttaCentral
PTSPali Text Society (from the 1880s)critical, European: from manuscripts of Sri Lanka, Myanmar and ThailandGRETIL

Column order: the national editions first, from fewer late texts in the Khuddaka Nikāya to more: Siam Rath has none of the Milindapañha, Nettippakaraṇa and Peṭakopadesa, Buddha Jayanti has the last two, Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana all three. Then the Mahāsaṅgīti, the printed one first, then SuttaCentral's version (the Myanmar text), and PTS last, a critical edition.

There are three national editions: SR, BJT, VRI. MS05 and MS are derived from the Myanmar one, which is why they are almost the same as VRI.

How to compare​

  • ✦ → Compare on any line opens the text with every edition and every translation in your languages, differences on; your own Compare settings stay as they were.
  • The Compare reader mode (Alt+Shift+2) shows by default every edition, a translation in your main language and the differences. What you change there (editions, lines or columns, translators, differences) is kept.
  • Choose the editions in Settings or the reader's quick settings. In columns (Alt+C), − / + remove or add a column.
  • The Differences switch highlights what most of the editions shown do not have.

Try it: sn56.11.
