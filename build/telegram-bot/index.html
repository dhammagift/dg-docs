Telegram bot

It's the same bot under two names — @Dhammagift_bot and its short form @Dgift_bot — same functionality, either one works.

It's an inline bot — you don't have to open a chat with it (though you can). The main way to use it: right in the message box of any chat or group, type @dgift_bot followed by a Pali word or a text number — Telegram immediately shows suggestions (words, links to texts) without leaving that chat. There are also dedicated Mini Apps: search, reading and the dictionary.
